# Studio USA — Imported Catalogue Pricing Guardrails

Internal working note. This file is not linked from the customer storefront.

## Principle

USA pricing for imported Art & Gifts and textile products is deliberately explicit and market-positioned. The Studio does **not** convert the European list price mechanically into USD.

The map should cover:
- materials and waste,
- handmade labor,
- customization time,
- packaging,
- payment/marketplace fees,
- overhead,
- seller-paid costs where applicable,
- a margin buffer appropriate for a handmade U.S. storefront.

Owner labor is a cost, not profit. Shipping is not silently assumed to be included unless a product-specific decision says so.

## Display rule

- Standard ready catalogue items show the listed price.
- Handmade made-to-order and custom-inquiry pieces display **From $X**.
- Future cost updates should change this explicit map only after reviewing the actual cost build.
- Never change the original Art & Gifts or Body Glow prices from Studio work.
