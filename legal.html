USA STORE · PRE-LAUNCH POLICIES

Launch gates before checkout opens.

This page records the current storefront rules. It is not a substitute for the final seller disclosures or product-specific compliance records.

Checkout remains locked. Legal operator identity, business/contact details, final product records and labels, U.S. shipping setup, and the intended LIVE PayPal credentials must be verified before STORE_LIVE=true.

Seller identification

Trading name: Velvet Charms Studio.

Legal operator, registered/professional address and fiscal/registration identifiers: not yet published; these fields must be completed and verified before public checkout. No placeholder is to be presented as a real legal identity.

USA storefront scope

This Studio storefront is intended for U.S. customers and uses USD. Server-side checkout rejects non-U.S. market requests. The EU Art & Gifts catalogue and the original Body Glow storefront are separate and do not feed this U.S. sale catalogue.

V1 product scope

V1 is limited to the approved water-free Body Butter, Nourishing Face Balm, Hand & Foot Balm, Solid Perfume and Perfume Oil Roll-On architecture plus glycerin/melt-and-pour soaps once their exact supplier base and compatible additive limits are documented. True water-based creams and candles are outside V1.

Product documentation and claims

Every sellable formula must retain its exact composition/version and relevant supplier documentation. Fragrance material restrictions and finished-product safety must be reviewed for the actual materials used. Store copy is limited to ordinary cosmetic functions such as cleansing, moisturizing, softening, conditioning and fragrancing; the storefront must not claim that an ordinary cosmetic is FDA Approved or make drug/medical treatment claims.

Prices and payment

Studio USA uses USD. Product identity, quantity, allowed customization and totals are validated server-side before PayPal order creation and again before capture. LIVE payment remains disabled until the intended PayPal account credentials are verified in the Studio deployment environment.

Shipping

Public checkout is intended for U.S. delivery only. Final shipping method, charges, import/customs handling and carrier eligibility must be configured before launch. Commercial merchandise must be described accurately in shipping/customs documentation.

Returns and order problems

Final cancellation, return, refund and damaged-order terms must be completed against the actual seller setup and applicable consumer rules before checkout opens. Product safety complaints and adverse-event reports require an accessible customer contact route and appropriate records.

Privacy and storage

The site uses browser local storage for the shopping bag and session storage for the temporary PayPal handoff. PayPal processes payment data under its own terms. Non-essential analytics or advertising tracking must not be added without reviewing the applicable privacy/consent setup.

Launch rule

None of the pre-launch wording on this page authorizes a sale by itself. The server launch gate remains the controlling technical protection until the outstanding legal, product, shipping and payment checks are completed.
