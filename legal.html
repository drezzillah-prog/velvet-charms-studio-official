SHOP POLICIES

Clear expectations for handmade orders.

These policies explain how Studio orders are handled. Mandatory consumer rights always prevail over any shop term that would conflict with them.

Pre-launch legal detail: the final PFA legal name, registration details, fiscal identification, professional address and official customer-contact details must be inserted here before STORE_LIVE=true is enabled. Checkout is intentionally server-gated until that launch step is complete.

Seller identification

Trading name: Velvet Charms Studio.

Legal operator: to be completed immediately after PFA registration.

Registered / professional address: to be completed before public checkout.

Fiscal / registration identifiers: to be completed before public checkout.

Customer contact: the Contact page will route messages to the official business inbox configured for launch.

Orders and handmade production

An order is accepted for production only when payment is confirmed and the requested customization is feasible. Handmade and commissioned pieces can have natural variations. Any requested date entered at checkout is a preference until confirmed against the production schedule and delivery route.

Prices and payment

The Studio checkout uses EUR as the PayPal settlement currency. Romanian catalogue prices may be displayed in RON alongside the corresponding Studio EUR checkout amount. The server validates the product, quantity, permitted customization values and order total before a PayPal order is created and checks the same cart again before payment capture.

Shipping

Shipping depends on destination, package size, weight and the nature of the handmade piece. Where shipping is not included in the product total, the shipping arrangement and any additional shipping payment are confirmed separately before dispatch. Customers receive the information necessary to understand the total delivery arrangement before the parcel is sent.

Cancellation, withdrawal and personalized goods

Consumer withdrawal rights are applied according to the law that governs the transaction. Goods made to a consumer's specifications or clearly personalized can fall within a statutory exception to the ordinary withdrawal right. This does not remove legal rights for goods that are defective, damaged, unsafe, incorrectly supplied or otherwise non-conforming.

Returns and problems

If a product arrives damaged, incorrect or materially different from the confirmed order, contact the Studio promptly with the order reference and relevant photos. The appropriate remedy is assessed under the applicable consumer rules. Customers should not discard damaged packaging before the issue has been documented when carrier damage may be involved.

Product safety

The public Studio checkout contains only the products specifically allowlisted for the first launch. Candles are displayed as a non-sale preview until their formulation-specific safety classification and label review is complete. Cosmetics are excluded from Studio checkout during the first phase. Products with separate electrical conformity work are also excluded from the Studio sale allowlist.

Privacy

The website processes the information necessary to answer messages and process orders. PayPal processes payment information under its own service terms and privacy framework. The Studio does not ask customers to send payment-card credentials through the contact form. Order and customer information should be retained only for legitimate business, legal, accounting and customer-service purposes and protected with appropriate access controls.

Cookies and local storage

The current Studio front end uses browser local storage for the shopping bag and session storage for the temporary PayPal checkout handoff. These storage mechanisms support functionality rather than advertising profiling. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking is added later, the consent setup must be reviewed before it is enabled.

Policy changes

The version displayed at the time of the order applies to that order together with mandatory law. Material changes are published on this page rather than silently rewriting an already-confirmed custom order.
