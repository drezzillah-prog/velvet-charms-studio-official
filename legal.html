SHOP POLICIES

What to expect when you order.

Clear information for catalogue pieces, custom work, payment and delivery.

Prices

Prices on this storefront are shown in U.S. dollars. Custom commissions, large quantities and specialist work may use a personalized quote instead of the standard catalogue price.

Handmade & made-to-order pieces

Small variations in texture, placement, color and finish can occur in handmade work. Made-to-order and custom pieces are created around the options agreed with you, so representative photos should be treated as examples rather than identical replicas.

Custom quotes

For custom wedding work, commissions, preservation pieces, bulk orders and selected catalogue items, we confirm the requested design, materials, quantity, timing and price before accepting the order.

Payment

Online payments are handled through PayPal when checkout is available. You will see the order total before approving payment. If online checkout is unavailable, contact us rather than sending payment independently.

Shipping

This storefront is intended for U.S. customers. Shipping method, timing and charges depend on the destination and the piece being ordered. Made-to-order and custom pieces can require additional production time.

Returns & order problems

Return eligibility can differ for personalized, made-to-order and standard catalogue pieces. The applicable terms for an order should be confirmed before payment. If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us as soon as possible with the order details and photos.

Privacy

The shopping bag may use browser storage so your selections remain available while you browse. PayPal processes payment information under its own privacy terms. Contact-form information is used to respond to your request.

Questions before ordering?

Please contact us. We would rather clarify a detail, show another example or prepare the right quote before a piece is made.
