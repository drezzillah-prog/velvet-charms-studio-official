VELVET CHARMS STUDIO

Questions, answered simply.

About the collection, personalization, custom work, shipping and ordering.

What can I find in the Studio catalogue?

Handmade art and portraits, hair accessories, resin and clay pieces, leather bags, gift sets, knitted and felted creations, pet wear, unscented body care, solid fragrance, soaps and selected made-to-order pieces from the wider Velvet Charms universe.

Are the body balms scented?

No. The Body Butter, Nourishing Face Balm and Hand & Foot Balm currently listed in the Studio are unscented, so there is no scent selector on those products.

What sizes are the solid perfumes?

The planned sizes are Net Wt. 0.18 oz / 5 g and Net Wt. 0.35 oz / 10 g.

Can I personalize a piece?

Many products include choices such as color palette, finish, personalization, gift wrap, hidden messages or other product-specific options. The available choices appear on each product.

What if I need something that is not listed exactly as I imagine it?

Contact us. Wedding pieces, commissions, place-based keepsakes and many Life Chapters objects can be discussed as custom work and quoted individually.

Can I ask for more example photos?

Yes. For made-to-order and highly customized work, the images shown are representative examples. Contact us if you would like additional examples before requesting a quote.

What does “Coming soon” mean?

The piece is part of the Studio collection but is not accepting orders yet. You can still open the product and ask to be notified.

What does “Custom inquiry” mean?

The piece is handled individually rather than through standard checkout. Contact us and we will confirm the design, options, timing and quote with you.

Where does the Studio ship?

This storefront is intended for customers in the United States. If you are outside the U.S., contact us before ordering so we can tell you which Velvet Charms storefront is appropriate.

How does payment work?

When online checkout is available, payment is handled securely through PayPal. If checkout is temporarily unavailable, you can still contact us about a product or custom piece.

Will a handmade or custom piece look exactly like the photo?

Not always. Small variations are part of handmade work, and custom commissions are created around the details agreed with you. Product photos are representative unless a listing states otherwise.
