QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Made by hand means every order has a little more story.

Here is what to expect when ordering a handmade or personalized Velvet Charms Studio piece.

Are the products handmade?

The Studio collection is built around handmade, small-batch and custom work. Small variations in handmade pieces are part of the character of the object rather than factory-style defects.

Can I customize a product?

Yes. Where a product has fixed choices, you can select them before adding it to your bag. You can also add special instructions. For commissioned art, reference details and final feasibility are confirmed before production begins.

How long does an order take?

Making time depends on the type and complexity of the piece. A requested gift or delivery date can be added during checkout, but it is a preference rather than a guaranteed date until the production slot and shipping route are confirmed.

How does payment work?

The Studio checkout uses PayPal. The order amount is validated against the Studio catalogue before the PayPal order is created, and the approved order is checked again before capture.

What about shipping?

Shipping depends on destination, size, weight and the nature of the handmade piece. Where the product payment does not include the final shipping cost, shipping is confirmed separately before dispatch. No parcel is sent without the destination and shipping arrangement being clear.

Why are candles visible but not purchasable?

The candle collection is intentionally shown as a preview while formulation-specific classification and safety-label information is finalized. The Studio does not enable a candle for checkout simply because it exists in the wider Velvet Charms catalogue.

Where are the creams, soaps and perfumes?

They belong to the complete Body Glow universe and are not sold through this first Studio launch collection. They will return in the dedicated Body Glow release after their product-specific cosmetic compliance work is complete.

Can a custom order be returned?

Personalized and made-to-order goods can be treated differently from standard goods under consumer law. Our final return rules depend on whether the item was made to your specifications, whether it is defective or non-conforming, and the legal rights that cannot be waived. See the Policies page for the final order rules in force at the time of purchase.
