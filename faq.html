USA V1 · PRELAUNCH

Questions about the first U.S. collection.

Velvet Charms Studio USA is being prepared as a separate U.S.-focused storefront. Checkout remains locked until the applicable product, business, label and payment launch gates are completed.

What is in USA V1?

The planned V1 families are Body Butter, Nourishing Face Balm, Hand & Foot Balm, Solid Perfume, Perfume Oil Roll-On, same-formula refills and glycerin/melt-and-pour soaps. Water-based creams and candles are outside this V1 launch.

Why are Face Balm and Hand & Foot Balm not called creams?

The current V1 development formulas are water-free balms. The storefront names them according to the product form actually being developed rather than presenting them as water-based creams.

Are all products orderable yet?

No. The storefront is in prelaunch. Supplier-dependent soap formulas are blocked until the exact documented base and compatible additive levels are frozen. Fragrance products are also blocked from server-side checkout while their fragrance/material documentation and applicable restrictions remain launch gates.

What happened to Citrus Bloom?

Citrus Bloom is deliberately not frozen into the orderable V1 catalogue yet. Its exact aromatic materials and leave-on suitability must be documented before a final formula is set.

Are these products FDA approved?

No such claim is made. The Studio does not describe its cosmetics as “FDA Approved.” Product safety substantiation, final labels and the applicable U.S. regulatory and operational requirements must be completed before sale.

What claims will the products make?

The intended positioning stays cosmetic: for example moisturizes, softens, conditions, cleanses or fragrances the skin where appropriate. The Studio does not use unsubstantiated medical or drug-treatment claims for these V1 cosmetics.

Where will Studio USA ship?

This storefront is being configured for customers in the United States and USD checkout. Non-U.S. requests are blocked by the server-side market gate for this launch architecture.

How will payment work?

The planned checkout uses PayPal. Cart contents and U.S. market eligibility are validated server-side before an order is created and checked again before capture. LIVE payment credentials and the final store-live gate remain disabled until launch readiness is verified.

Is this the same shop as Body Glow or Art & Gifts?

No. Velvet Charms Studio USA is a separate storefront. The existing Body Glow and Art & Gifts catalogues remain separate from this USA V1 architecture.

What are the final return and shipping terms?

Those public commercial terms will be finalized before checkout is enabled. The prelaunch site does not invent a seller identity, fiscal details, shipping promise or return rule that has not yet been established.
