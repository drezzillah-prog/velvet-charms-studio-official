SAMPLE WAITLIST

FROM BLOTTER TO BOTTLE

Made by iteration, not by vibes.

01

Verify

Exact materials and supplier information are checked before a development batch is treated as a formula input.

02

Weigh

Development work is recorded by mass and version so a promising result can actually be reproduced.

03

Blotter

Opening, heart and drydown are observed over time before any version earns the next round.

04

Change one thing

One meaningful variable moves at a time. A new direction becomes a new version, not an improvised correction.

05

Live with it

Versions rest and are revisited. The goal is to choose what deserves to survive, not force every idea to work.

06

Clear before skin

If the relevant safety and documentation work is not ready, the scent remains a blotter preview rather than a skin sample.

LAB JOURNAL · EDITED FOR THE STUDIO

What changed — without publishing the formula.

IVORY HOUR

If the aromatic lift gets too loud, the brief returns to softness before adding anything new.

VEILED

If rose or jasmine announces itself immediately, the floral volume comes down. Mystery wins.

BLACK HONEY

If sweetness turns edible, the direction moves toward wood and resin rather than adding more spice.

SACRED SMOKE

If the resin becomes too heavy, the darker materials are reduced before sweetness is used as a fix.
