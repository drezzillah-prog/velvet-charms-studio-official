# Studio USA — Image Audit

Internal working note. Not linked from the customer storefront.

## Current coverage

The full Studio catalogue is required by the live contract to have at least one image for every customer-facing product. The current catalogue contains 64 pieces and the integrity test rejects any product with an empty image array.

The four Velvet Classics solid perfumes use Studio-owned imagery:
- IVORY HOUR
- VEILED
- BLACK HONEY
- SACRED SMOKE

## Image strategy

Do not generate a unique gallery for every made-to-order variation. The storefront should use:
- one strong primary image for every listed product,
- existing source galleries where good images already exist,
- one or two representative examples for highly customizable work,
- additional examples on request for wedding, preservation, place-based and bespoke commissions.

## Highest-value future photography / generation

Prioritize only products where another image materially improves conversion:
1. Leather bags — only one existing image per size.
2. Couple/family portrait and mini portrait — one existing example each.
3. 3D festive portrait — one existing example.
4. Braided blankets — one image per size.
5. Felted animals — one image for most formats.
6. Category-level lifestyle imagery for Art & Gifts, Textiles & Comfort and Body Care.

## Do not overproduce

Products with established multi-image galleries (hair accessories, resin/clay objects, phone cases and several portraits) do not need new imagery simply to increase image count.

Life Chapters should continue to use representative examples plus the customer-facing contact route for more photos and personalized quotations.
